      - Manages obstacle stream approaching along Z-axis (from horizon `z = 1.0` down to `z = 0.0`).
      - Obstacle Types:
        - `asteroid`: Rock obstacle with collision radius, deals 1 shield damage.
        - `plasma-mine`: Explosive mine, deals 2 shield damage (or 1 + heavy shock).
        - `boost-ring`: Glowing energy gate/ring, stepping through awards +500 pts, triggers ship boost (3s), and recovers 1 shield HP.
      - Properties per obstacle: `id`, `type`, `x` (lane position -1.0 to 1.0), `z` (1.0 to -0.2), `radius`, `rotation`, `rotationSpeed`, `cleared`, `collided`.
      - Spawning: dynamically spaces obstacles based on current track speed, ensures at least one navigable gap across lanes.
      - Collision / interaction check with Ship at `z <= 0.08 && z >= -0.05`.
    - GameState:
      - Status machine: `ready` -> `playing` -> `paused` -> `gameover`.
      - State metrics: `distance`, `speed`, `score`, `highScore`, `shields`, `boostTimeRemaining`, `gatesCleared`, `asteroidsDodged`, `nearMisses`.
      - Game over triggered when `shields <= 0`.
      - High score loaded from and saved to storage (`arcade-carnival-space-racer-highscore`) via `@arcade-carnival/playables-adapter`.
  </behavior>
