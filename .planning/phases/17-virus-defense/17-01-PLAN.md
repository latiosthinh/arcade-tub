      - Damage & Repair: `takeDamage(amount: number): boolean` decreases HP (clamped to 0), `heal(amount: number)` restores HP (clamped to maxHp).
      - Antibodies: `spawnAntibody()` spawns a floating repair orb orbiting/drifting near nucleus; shooting or colliding with it heals 15 HP.
      - Wave scaling: `getWaveConfig(wave: number)` returns enemy count, speed multiplier, spawn interval, and pathogen type distribution.
