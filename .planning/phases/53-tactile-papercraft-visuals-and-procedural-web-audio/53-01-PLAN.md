  <files>games/tank-1990/src/types.ts, games/tank-1990/src/ParticleEmitter.ts</files>

    1. In `games/tank-1990/src/types.ts`, export:
       - `ParticleType`: `'CONFETTI' | 'DEBRIS' | 'SPARK' | 'DUST' | 'SMOKE'`
       - `Particle` interface:
         - `x: number`, `y: number`, `vx: number`, `vy: number`
         - `size: number`, `width: number`, `height: number`
         - `rotation: number`, `vRot: number`
         - `color: string`, `alpha: number`
         - `life: number`, `maxLife: number`
         - `type: ParticleType`
         - `gravity?: number`, `drag?: number`
    2. Create `games/tank-1990/src/ParticleEmitter.ts`:
       - Class `ParticleEmitter`:
         - Holds active particles array `particles: Particle[] = []`.
         - Particle capacity cap (e.g. 250 particles) to maintain high performance.
         - `emitExplosion(x: number, y: number, isBig: boolean = false)`: Spawns 20–35 multicolored cardboard confetti rectangles (papercraft palette: red `#e74c3c`, yellow `#f1c40f`, blue `#3498db`, green `#2ecc71`, orange `#e67e22`, white `#ecf0f1`) with radial burst velocity, random angular rotation velocities, air drag, and gentle downward gravity.
         - `emitBrickDebris(x: number, y: number, count: number = 8)`: Spawns small brown/terracotta crumbling squares with gravity.
         - `emitSparks(x: number, y: number, count: number = 6)`: Spawns high-velocity yellow/white spark streaks with short lifespans (0.15–0.25s).
         - `emitTreadDust(x: number, y: number)`: Spawns soft expanding grey/sand dust circles that drift and fade quickly.
         - `update(dt: number)`: Advances particle positions (`x += vx * dt`, `y += vy * dt`), applies gravity/drag, increments rotation, decays life, and computes alpha fade (`alpha = life / maxLife`). Filters out dead particles.
         - `render(ctx: CanvasRenderingContext2D)`: Draws all active particles with appropriate transforms (`translate`, `rotate`, `globalAlpha`), rendering confetti as paper rectangles, debris as irregular squares, sparks as glowing lines/diamonds, and dust as soft circles. Restores context state.
         - `clear()`: Clears active particle pool.
  </action>
