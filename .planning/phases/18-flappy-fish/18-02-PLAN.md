      - Methods:
        - `emitFlapBubbles(x: number, y: number, count: number)`: Emits small ascending bubble particles with buoyant upward deceleration.
        - `emitPearlSparkles(x: number, y: number, count: number)`: Emits radial golden/cyan sparkling starburst particles.
        - `emitCrashDebris(x: number, y: number, count: number)`: Emits colorful coral/fin fragment particles with gravitational decay.
        - `update(dt: number)`: Advances particle kinematics, buoyancy drift, alpha fade, and culls expired particles.
        - `render(ctx: CanvasRenderingContext2D)`: Draws particles with appropriate glow and blending.
    - FishAudio:
      - Uses `AudioSynthesizer` / `AudioContext` to generate procedural sound effects without external assets:
        - `playFlap()`: Low-to-high frequency pitch swoop with gentle bubble filter (`bloop`).
        - `playScore()`: Bright two-tone harmonic chime (`C6` -> `G6`).
        - `playPearl()`: High-pitched crystalline resonant ping (`E6` -> `B6` -> `E7`).
        - `playCrash()`: Low-frequency noise burst + downward bass thud.
  </behavior>
