  <behavior>
    - CarAudio:
      - Uses AudioContext / Web Audio API with zero external dependencies.
      - Continuous engine sound: looped oscillator with dynamic frequency pitch bend mapped to player speed (e.g. 55Hz at 100 km/h up to 240Hz at 350 km/h) and harmonic overtone gain.
      - Methods:
        - `startEngine()`: Initializes continuous engine oscillator node with low-pass filter and gain ramp.
        - `stopEngine()`: Stops or mutes engine loop.
        - `updateEngineSpeed(speed: number, minSpeed: number = 100, maxSpeed: number = 350)`: Modulates engine oscillator frequency and distortion/filter cutoff in real-time based on speed fraction.
        - `playTireScreech()`: High-pitched friction noise sweep (e.g. 900Hz-1400Hz frequency modulation with bandpass filter, 0.15s duration).
        - `playSlipstreamWhoosh()`: Resonant airy whoosh when drafting (pink/white noise burst with bandpass sweep).
        - `playPoliceSiren()`: Alternating two-tone siren (600Hz / 900Hz square wave oscillation).
        - `playCrash()`: Deep explosive collision boom with noise transient and low bass rumble (160Hz -> 25Hz sweep, 0.5s duration).
        - `setMuted(muted: boolean): void`.
    - ParticleSystem:
      - Manages pool of particles (position `x, y`, velocity `vx, vy`, `color`, `size`, `alpha`, `life`, `maxLife`, `shape`).
      - Methods:
        - `emitExhaust(x: number, y: number, isBoosting: boolean)`: Dual cyan/orange exhaust flame and smoke particles trailing behind player car.
        - `emitSpeedLines(count?: number)`: Vertical neon motion blur streaks rushing downwards along highway edges.
        - `emitDraftStreamlines(x: number, y: number)`: Glowing cyan aerodynamic draft stream particles flanking player car when in slipstream.
        - `emitTireSmoke(x: number, y: number)`: White/grey rubber smoke puffs when braking or sharp cornering.
        - `emitCrashExplosion(x: number, y: number)`: Fiery explosion shockwave, orange/yellow fire particles, black smoke, and glowing car shrapnel.
        - `update(dt: number)`: Updates positions, drag, alpha fade, and culls expired particles.
        - `reset()`: Clears particle pool.
  </behavior>
