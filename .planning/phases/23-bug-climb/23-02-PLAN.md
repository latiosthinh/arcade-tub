  <behavior>
    - ClimbAudio:
      - Uses AudioContext / Web Audio API.
      - Methods:
        - `playStep(side: string)`: Crisp percussive tap/scurry click (e.g. 500Hz/650Hz pitch variation alternating left/right, 0.04s duration).
        - `playChop()`: Snappy wood crack/chop whoosh sound on trunk advancement (e.g. 280Hz -> 110Hz downward pitch sweep with low-pass filtered transient).
        - `playComboChime(streak: number)`: Ascending harmonic chime scaling with combo multiplier (e.g. 520Hz + 100Hz * multiplier, 0.12s duration).
        - `playUrgentTick()`: Tense warning beep (880Hz square/sine pulse, 0.05s).
        - `playCrash()`: Deep wood collision crunch and branch snap noise (180Hz down to 30Hz with noise burst, 0.4s).
        - `setMuted(muted: boolean): void`.
    - ParticleSystem:
      - Manages pool of particles (position `x, y`, velocity `vx, vy`, `color`, `size`, `alpha`, `life`, `maxLife`, `rotation`, `vRot`).
      - Methods:
        - `emitWoodChips(x: number, y: number, side: 'LEFT' | 'RIGHT', count?: number)`: Radial spray of flying brown/amber wood chips.
        - `emitLeaves(x: number, y: number, count?: number)`: Green fluttering foliage leaf debris.
        - `emitStreakSparkles(x: number, y: number, count?: number)`: Golden/cyan combo score sparkles floating upwards.
        - `emitCrashBurst(x: number, y: number)`: High velocity explosion of wood splinters and red warning sparks.
        - `update(dt: number)`: Updates position with gravity/drag, alpha fade, rotation, and dead particle culling.
        - `reset()`: Clears particle pool.
  </behavior>

       - Particle pool initialization and capacity limits.

       - Particle movement, alpha fade, rotation physics, and removal on expiration.
       - Particle pool reset.
