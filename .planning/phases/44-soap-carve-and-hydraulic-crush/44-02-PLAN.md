  <behavior>
    - PistonPhysics:
      - `constructor(maxStroke = 300, maxForce = 1000)`: tracks piston position (y), velocity, applied force, hydraulic pressure (0 to 1000 bar / PSI), and resistance from target item.
      - `applyPressure(holding, itemStiffness, dt)`: when `holding` is true, advances piston downwards against item resistance; increases pressure gauge reading as item compresses; retracts smoothly upwards when input released.
      - `getDisplacement()`: returns current normalized compression height (0.0 = top/resting, 1.0 = bottom anvil limit).
      - `getPressure()`: returns current pressure reading in bar.
    - CrushItems:
      - Item registry: `duck` (squishy rubber, low yield), `can` (metallic accordion, mid yield), `clock` (fragile plastic gears, mid yield), `watermelon` (explosive juicy fruit, low yield), `slime` (hyper-elastic goo, high yield), `diamond` (unbreakable hydraulic stall test).
      - `getDeformation(item, displacement)`: calculates vertical compression factor `scaleY`, horizontal accordion bulge `scaleX` (volume preservation `scaleX ≈ 1 / sqrt(scaleY)`), accordion wrinkle count, and crushed state boolean (`isCrushed: boolean`).
      - `checkYieldCollapse(item, pressure, displacement)`: returns true if item has exceeded yield strength and entered permanent structural collapse.
    - CrushSplatter:
      - `spawnSplatter(originX, originY, color, particleCount, explosionForce, isJuicy)`: creates droplets and chunky debris that radiate outwards horizontally and splat onto anvil and press chamber walls.
      - `update(dt)`: integrates gravity, ground friction, and sticky wall splat stains.
    - HydraulicCrush.test.ts:
      - Piston rests at 0 position and advances downward under applied input.
      - Resistance from stiffer items slows piston descent and spikes pressure.
      - Item deformation preserves approximate area/volume with outward bulge.
      - Yield collapse triggers accurately when pressure/compression exceeds item threshold.
      - Splatter particles emit in horizontal radial burst with proper velocities and lifetime decay.
      - Diamond item resists full force and causes hydraulic pressure stall without collapsing.
  </behavior>
