# Plan: Phase 36-01 - 12 MiniBattles Party Duel Engine

## Task 1: Party Engine, 12 Mini-Game Modes & Unit Tests
Create `games/mini-battles/package.json`, `games/mini-battles/tsconfig.json`, `games/mini-battles/src/PartyEngine.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/GameModes.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/GameState.ts`.
- 1-Button 2-Player (Local P1 vs P2) or 1P vs CPU party system.
- P1 input (W / Tap Left), P2 input (Up Arrow / Tap Right).
- 12 distinct mini-battle modes implemented:
  1. Paper Duel (quick-draw shoot)
  2. Cardboard Tug of War (rapid tap pull)
  3. Table Soccer (spin kick paper ball)
  4. Lava Hop (tap to leap rising cardboard platforms)
  5. Balloon Pop (tap pump until opponent balloon pops)
  6. Tank Clash (rotate & shoot craft paper cannon)
  7. Sumotori (bump opponent off circular mat)
  8. Laser Dodge (jump over rotating laser bar)
  9. Coin Snatch (rush center coin first)
  10. Knife Flip (stick cardboard dart into target)
  11. Helicopter Drop (tap to flap rotors onto landing pad)
  12. Hammer Smash (whack popup cardboard critter)
- Unit tests in `games/mini-battles/test/` for each minigame win condition, input processing, round scoring, and CPU AI decision logic.

## Task 2: Papercraft Duel Renderer, Party Audio, Scene & Shell
Create `games/mini-battles/src/BattleRenderer.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/BattleParticles.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/BattleAudio.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/MiniBattlesScene.ts`, `games/mini-battles/src/main.ts`, and `games/mini-battles/index.html`.
- 2D Papercraft puppet theater style, cutout cardboard gladiators, vibrant textured backdrops.
- Web Audio synthesizer for fanfare, whistles, cannon bangs, pops, cheers, countdown beeps.
- HUD with mode carousel, round stars / match score (first to 5 wins), mode selector and CPU difficulty toggle.
