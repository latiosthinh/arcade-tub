      - `playBombExplosion()`: low-frequency rumble + white noise burst for explosive blast.
    - ParticleSystem:
      - Emits colorful confetti sparkle shards and glowing ring shockwaves on balloon pop.
      - Emits fiery ember particles and smoke puffs on bomb detonation.
    - FloatingScore:
      - Spawns floating text elements at pop location `(x, y)` showing points awarded (e.g. `+300 x2.5!`).
      - Drifts upward and fades opacity over 0.8s.
    - Tests verify particle updates/lifecycles and floating score drift/fading.
  </behavior>

         - Draws balloon knot and main oval body with glossy highlights.
         - If rainbow: computes dynamic hue shift or gradient.
         - If bomb: draws spiked perimeter and pulsating center core.
