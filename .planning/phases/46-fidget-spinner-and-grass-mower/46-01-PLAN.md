  <behavior>
    - SpinnerPhysics:
      - `constructor(config?: Partial<SpinnerConfig>)`: initializes angle (radians), angularVelocity (rad/s), bearingFriction (damping factor), totalRevolutions, coins, and bearing level.
      - `applyTorque(impulse)`: adds instantaneous rotational velocity (clamped to max angular velocity).
      - `applySwipe(p1, p2, dt, center)`: computes tangential swipe force relative to spinner center: cross product `(r x v) / |r|` to calculate angular impulse.
      - `update(dt)`: applies friction deceleration `angularVelocity *= Math.pow(bearingFriction, dt * 60)`. When `|angularVelocity| < 0.01`, snap to 0. Updates `angle = (angle + angularVelocity * dt) % (2 * Math.PI)`. Accumulates `totalRevolutions += Math.abs(angularVelocity * dt) / (2 * Math.PI)`.
      - `getRPM()`: returns `(Math.abs(angularVelocity) / (2 * Math.PI)) * 60`.
      - `upgradeBearing()`: unlocks smoother ceramic/hybrid/magnetic bearings that reduce friction factor (e.g. from 0.985 to 0.998).
    - TrailRenderer:
      - `addPoint(tipIndex, x, y, speedRatio)`: stores historic tip positions with timestamp and intensity.
      - `render(ctx, color)`: draws glowing neon bezier arcs trailing behind spinner blade tips with alpha decay.
      - `spawnSparks(x, y, count)`: generates glitter sparks on ultra-fast spins (RPM > 1000).
    - SpinnerAudio:
      - `updateHum(rpm, maxRpm)`: adjusts oscillator pitch (50Hz base humming up to 800Hz high whine) and gain based on RPM. Uses harmonic overtone oscillator for authentic metallic/ceramic bearing resonance.
      - `playSwipeWhoosh(intensity)`: plays short filtered white noise sweep on fast swipe.
      - `playUpgradeSound()`: joyful chime on bearing level upgrade.
    - Unit Tests (`FidgetSpin.test.ts`):
      - Test swipe torque converts tangent velocity into angular velocity.
      - Test rotational deceleration correctly dampens angular velocity over time.
      - Test higher bearing upgrades exhibit lower friction / longer spin duration.
      - Test RPM math accurately converts rad/s to revolutions per minute.
      - Test revolution counter accumulates continuous rotations without precision loss.
  </behavior>
