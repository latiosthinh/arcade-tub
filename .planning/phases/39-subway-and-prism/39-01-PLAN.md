# Plan: Phase 39-01 - Subway Runner & Prism Laser

## Task 1: Subway Runner (`games/subway-runner/`)
Create package.json, tsconfig.json, src/LaneRunnerEngine.ts, src/TrainTrackGenerator.ts, src/GameState.ts, src/SubwayRenderer.ts, src/SubwayAudio.ts, src/SubwayScene.ts, src/main.ts, index.html, and unit tests in test/.
- 3-Lane endless vertical paper runner inspired by Subway Surfers.
- Controls: Swipe/Keys Left/Right (lane change), Up (Jump over barriers), Down (Slide under low signal boards).
- Oncoming cardboard commuter trains, road barriers, construction paper coin trails, hover-board origami powerup.
- Full Vitest suite for lane kinematics, jump/slide state machine, train collisions, coin magnets, and multiplier progression.

## Task 2: Prism Laser (`games/prism-laser/`)
Create package.json, tsconfig.json, src/OpticsEngine.ts, src/PuzzleGridGenerator.ts, src/GameState.ts, src/LaserRenderer.ts, src/LaserAudio.ts, src/PrismLaserScene.ts, src/main.ts, index.html, and unit tests in test/.
- Optics puzzle game reflecting, refracting, and filtering colored laser beams onto target paper crystals.
- Grid board with draggable / rotatable paper mirrors (45-degree angled cardstock reflectors), splitters (prisms dividing light into dual beams), and colored filters (converting white beam to RGB).
- Procedural puzzle levels with increasing complexity and move par.
- Full Vitest suite for beam tracing math (angle of reflection, refraction splitting, filter tinting), target activation detection, and level solvability verification.
