# Phase 31 Context: Paper Basket (Tap-Tap Shots Replication)

## Goal
Implement `games/paper-basket/` in the 2D Papercraft aesthetic.
Tap/Space/Click gives an upward parabolic arc impulse to a crumpled paper basketball.
Player scores by dropping the ball into cardboard hoop rims with net strings.
Hoops alternate left/right sides with randomized heights and moving motion as score increases.
Includes shot clock timer per hoop, streak multipliers for clean "swish" shots, procedural Web Audio, and unit tests.
