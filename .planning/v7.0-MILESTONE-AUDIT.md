## Summary
Milestone v7.0 expanded ArcadeTub from 34 games to **42 games** by creating and integrating 8 dedicated sensory antistress sandbox minigames with procedural ASMR Web Audio, zero cognitive load, and full PWA/fullscreen integration.

## Implemented Antistress Minigames Verification (8 New Games)

1. **Bubble Wrap Pop (`games/bubble-pop/`):**
   - Perforated papercraft bubble grid with popping physics and individual bubble state tracking.
   - Multi-touch swipe/drag popping and cursor sweeping.
   - Golden rainbow pop bubbles triggering harmonic chord cascades.
   - Infinite sheet regeneration with instant reset.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-01)*

2. **Pop-It Fidget Toy (`games/pop-it/`):**
   - 4 geometric silicone board shapes (Heart, Star, Hexagon, Bear) with 2-way dimple buttons.
   - Tactile convex/concave dimple audio and physics.
   - 3D board flip animation upon clearing all dimples.
   - Multi-color pastel and rainbow themes.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-06)*

3. **Soap Carver (`games/soap-carve/`):**
   - 3D layered pastel soap bar and wood block carving with drag slice gestures.
   - Shaving curl physics peeling off with procedural scraping/cutting audio.
   - Progressive carving depth revealing hidden collectible origami figurines.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-02)*

4. **Hydraulic Press (`games/hydraulic-crush/`):**
   - Hydraulic stamping press operated via holding click/space/touch.
   - Squishy papercraft targets (Rubber Duck, Clock, Can, Watermelon, Diamond).
   - Elastic accordion flattening animation, splatter particle burst, and hydraulic hiss audio.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-08)*

5. **Sand Zen Sandbox (`games/sand-zen/`):**
   - Interactive granular physics simulation with falling multi-color glowing sand streams.
   - Zen rake, color hoppers, sand funnels, and clear basin tools.
   - Natural dune angle-of-repose physics and satisfying granular avalanches.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-03)*

6. **Color Water Sort (`liquid-sort`):**
   - Test tubes with layered colored liquid pigments.
   - Viscous fluid pouring mechanics with color matching and tube capacity validation.
   - Stratification checks, undo move stack, level generation, and soothing liquid glug audio.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-05)*

7. **Fidget Spinner Speed (`fidget-spin`):**
   - Angular momentum physics solver with low bearing friction and inertia.
   - Swipe velocity tracker with real-time tachometer RPM display, max speed, and total revolutions.
   - Neon blade trails and dynamic harmonic hum pitch scaling with RPM.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-04)*

8. **Grass Mower Swirl (`grass-mow`):**
   - Steerable mini cardboard lawn mower with smooth directional touch / arrow controls.
   - Layered paper grass blades sliced into airborne confetti ribbons.
   - Mowing percentage tracker with garden maze patterns and buzzing engine Web Audio.
   - *Status: PASSED (FR-07)*
